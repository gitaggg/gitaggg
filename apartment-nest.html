Your Peaceful Sanctuary with Endless Sunlight

Peaceful Nest is a very spacious, bright room flooded with natural sunlight all day long. Every window opens wide to let cool mountain air flow through, creating a serene sanctuary for those seeking tranquility. Comfortable sofa, warm tea table, and a fully-equipped kitchen with all basic utensils await—no hidden charges, no utility bills for normal uses.

This is your very private escape, away from noise and chaos. The open windows stay brilliantly bright throughout the day, filling every corner with natural light and peaceful energy. While you have full rooftop access with stunning mountain views, peaceful Stupa vistas, and paragliding views, the focus here is pure, undisturbed comfort.

Located in a prime, guest-favorite area near bakery, gym, park, lake, restaurants, and fresh markets. Pokhara is a valley surrounded by green forest hills, less than 1km from Fewa Lake and a massive public green park right beside the water. Perfect for solo travelers and remote workers seeking peace and natural beauty.
